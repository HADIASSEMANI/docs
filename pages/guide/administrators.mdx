# Wave Connect Introductory Guides

## How to Set Owner Status

1. Navigate to the '_Administrators_' tab under your organization settings on Wave. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/admin-tab.png" alt="Navigate to Admin Tab" width="500px" />

    <Callout type="info">
    **Note**: Only administators with'_Owner_' priviledges have access to organization settings.
    </Callout><br />

2. Click on the '__Edit__' icon next to the admin you wish to assign as _Owner_. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/edit-button.png" alt="Edit Button Location" width="500px" />
    <br />

3. Set user's role to '_Owner_' then press '__Save__.' <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/save-admin.png" alt="Save Changes" width="500px" />
    <br />

4. New owner will receive a confirmation email in their mailbox. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/confirmation-email-owner.png" alt="Confirmation Email" width="500px" />
    <br />

## How to Set Admin Status

1. Navigate to the '_Administrators_' tab under your organization settings on Wave. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/admin-tab.png" alt="Navigate to Admin Tab" width="500px" />

    <Callout type="info">
    **Note**: Only administrators with '_Owner_' priviledges have access to organization settings.
    </Callout><br />

2. Click on the '__Add Admin__' button below your organization's list of admin team. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/add-admin.png" alt="Click on Add Admin" width="500px" />
    <br />

3. Select user you wish to provide admin rights to, set user's role to '_Admin_' then press '__Add__.' <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/select-user-admin.png" alt="Select User to Admin" width="500px" />
    <br />

4. New admin will receive a confirmation email in their mailbox. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/confirmation-email-admin.png" alt="Confirmation Email" width="500px" />
    <br />

## How to Add a User to Your Organization

1. Navigate your organization's '_Users_' tab on Wave. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/users-tab.png" alt="Navigate to Users Tab" width="500px" /><br />

2. Click on '__Create User__.'<br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/create-user.png" alt="Click on Create User" width="500px" /><br />

3. Enter user information per your organization's requirements. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/enter-user-info.png" alt="Enter User Information" width="500px" />
    <br />

4. Assign user to a team then press '__Create__.' <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/assign-team.png" alt="Assign User to a Team" width="500px" />
    <br />

5. New user will receive an invitation email in their mailbox. <br /><br />

    <img src="/security/access-control/get-started-email.png" alt="Confirmation Email" width="500px" />
    <br />
