## Introduction

In this document, we provide a step-by-step guide on how users can integrate Dynamics365 in their Wave account to sync contacts across their Dynamics365 ecosystem.

## Integrating Dynamics365 in Your Wave Account

1.  [Login](https://app.wavecnct.com/login) to your Wave user account
2.  Go to Settings, then click on Integrations.
3.  Click on the '**Connect**' button.<br /><br />
    <img src="/platform/integrations/dynamics-365/connect-dynamics.png" alt="Connect Dynamics" width="500px" />
    <br />
4.  A pop-up will open, allowing you to turn on contact auto sync between your Wave and Dynamics365 accounts. We recommend leaving this on. Press '**Continue**.' Additionally, provide your Dynamics365 Sales Domain in the space provided. A new tab will open asking you to authenticate your Dynamics365 account.<br /><br />
    <img
      src="/platform/integrations/dynamics-365/dynamics-auto-sync-contacts.png"
      alt="Toggle Auto Sync"
      width="400px"
    />
    <br />
5.  Provide your Microsoft Dynamics365 credentials in the fields provided. Press '**Log In**.'
    <Callout type="info">
      **Note**: The account you authenticate must be a Dynamics365 administrative account to ensure your integration
      works properly.
    </Callout>
    <br />
6.  After reviewing the permission prompt, check the consent box then press '**Accept**' to grant Dynamics365 access to your Wave account data. You will now be re-directed to your Wave account.<br /><br />
    <img src="/platform/integrations/dynamics-365/dynamics-allow-access.png" alt="Allow Access" width="300px" />
    <br />
7.  Verify a successful integration of Dynamics365 by spotting the ‘**Disconnect**’ button across Dynamics365.<br /><br />

    <img src="/platform/integrations/dynamics-365/disconnect-dynamics.png" alt="Disconnect Button" width="500px" />

    <Callout type="info">
      **Note**: Your Wave contacts will automatically sync as '**Contacts**' in your Dynamics365 account.
    </Callout>

Congratulations 🎉, you have successfully integrated Dynamics365 in your Wave account.

### Contact Owner Assignment:

Wave Connect automatically assigns Lead/Contact owner based on the creator's email. If the email does not match with any Dynamics365 user, the default value specified in your Dynamics365 account applies.
