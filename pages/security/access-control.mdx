# Access control

Explore roles, permissions, and access control options for your organization

## Roles and permissions

We currently support three different roles in your organization:

- Organization Owner
- Organization Administrator
- Organization Member

### Organization Owner

The owner has full control over an organization. They can add or remove members, change team settings, and manage administratives and security aspects of the organization.

### Organization Administrator

Admininstrators have access to teams settings such as interface templates and customer relationship management (CRM). Unlike owners they do no have access to the organization's settings.

### Organization Member

Users only have access to their Wave user account. Unlike _Owner_ and _Admins_, they do not have access to the organization's teams page.
